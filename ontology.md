# Ontology Summary

This ontology defines a set of relationship types (specs) that describe how various business, economic, and organizational entities interact. Each spec (relationship type) includes a description, example triples, and rules about which entity kinds can be connected via that relationship. Key relationship types include:

- **Has**: Ownership or possession (e.g., "apple HAS iphone").
  - Companies, organizations, people, or governments can "have" products, financial instruments, or economic indicators.

- **Announce**: Public statements or declarations (e.g., "federal reserve ANNOUNCE interest rate decision").
  - Companies, organizations, regulatory bodies, people, and governments announce events or products.

- **Operate In**: Presence in a sector or geography (e.g., "apple OPERATE_IN china").
  - Companies, organizations, and people operate in geopolitical entities or sectors.

- **Introduce**: Launch of new products/services (e.g., "apple INTRODUCE vision pro").
  - Companies, organizations, and people can introduce products.

- **Produce**: Manufacturing or creation (e.g., "intel PRODUCE semiconductors").
  - Companies and organizations produce products.

- **Control**: Regulation or ownership/control (e.g., "meta CONTROL instagram").
  - Regulatory bodies, governments, companies, people, and organizations can control companies, organizations, products, or economic indicators.

- **Participates In**: Engagement in events or platforms (e.g., "apple PARTICIPATES_IN consumer electronics show").
  - Companies, organizations, and people participate in events or with products.

- **Impact / PositiveImpactOn / NegativeImpactOn**: Effect (positive, negative, or neutral) on other entities (e.g., "recession IMPACT stock market").
  - Events, regulatory bodies, governments, economic indicators, products, companies, people, and organizations may impact companies, sectors, instruments, products, or indicators.

- **Relate To**: General association (e.g., "analyst report RELATE_TO company").
  - Used for broad connections between a wide range of entity types.

- **Is Member Of**: Membership, employment, or affiliation (e.g., "elon musk IS_MEMBER_OF tesla").
  - People can be members of companies, organizations, or governments; companies can be members of organizations or sectors.

- **Invests In**: Investment or allocation of capital (e.g., "warren buffett INVESTS_IN aapl").
  - People, companies, and organizations invest in companies, financial instruments, sectors, organizations, or products.

- **Increase / Decrease**: Raising or lowering indicators (e.g., "federal reserve RAISE interest rates").
  - Governments, regulatory bodies, organizations, companies, and people can increase/decrease economic indicators.

- **Competes With**: Market competition (e.g., "apple COMPETES_WITH samsung").
  - Companies, organizations, products, or people can compete with others of the same kind.

Each relationship type has specific rules about which *kinds* of entities (company, product, sector, person, etc.) can be subjects or objects in that relationship. This enables structured, semantically rich connections in a knowledge graph for business and economic data.
